# Studio and commercial licensing

Shotrover is **free** for individuals, hobbyists, students, non-profits and public institutions
under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE).

**Freelancers use it free too.** If you work on your own account (a freelancer, a sole trader, or a
one-person company), you can use Shotrover for paid work on your own computers without buying
anything. This is the additional permission at the top of the [LICENSE](LICENSE) file.

**Companies and studios need a studio licence.** That covers anyone using Shotrover as an employee,
and anyone, freelancers included, using it on computers the studio provides or manages.

- usable on any number of machines, at any of the studio's locations

## After you buy

The licence PDF arrives with your Ko-fi receipt. **Keep both on file**: together they are your proof
of licence. If you bought on behalf of a studio and the receipt shows your own name, reply to the
receipt with the studio's name and you'll get written confirmation for your records.

Need an invoice for your accounts team, a purchase order, or a larger agreement? Open an issue and
we'll sort it out.

## Support and custom work

The licence covers use of the software. Support contracts, pipeline integration and custom
previewers can be arranged separately: [open an issue](https://github.com/KCArrow/shotrover/issues).
Bug reports are welcome from everyone, licensed or not: Help → Report a problem in Shotrover.
